# Bullwark product requirements

Bullwark is the control room for a small finance team's AI. Its requirements are in three documents:

| Document | Covers | Main owners |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [`BULLWARK-PRD.md`](BULLWARK-PRD.md) | The product and its frontend: problem, persona, moderator console, member view, presentation rules, demo, sponsor tracks, positioning | C (frontend), B (story) |
| [`CANON-PRD.md`](CANON-PRD.md) | The agentic backend: capture, facts and groups, tiers, scopes, handoffs, agents, MCP, security, benchmark | A (server), B (capture and agents) |
| [`INTEGRATION.md`](INTEGRATION.md) | The only interface between them: REST, server-sent events, tokens and visibility rules | A and C |

Ownership and build order are in [`WORK_SPLIT.md`](WORK_SPLIT.md).
