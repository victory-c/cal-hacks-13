Research desk note. Public sources only; nothing here comes from the deal side.

- Nimbus pre-announced Q3 on 10/16 (8-K): revenue ~$1.02bn vs. ~$1.05bn consensus. Brokerage
  softer, LTL tonnage +2% y/y. Full results 11/6.
- Stock fell 7% on the pre-announcement to $22.80, then climbed back to $24.10 by 10/21 on
  above-average volume (roughly 2.3x the 30-day average on 10/20 and 10/21). Nothing on the tape
  explains the bounce.
- One sell-side shop cut to Hold (PT $25 from $29): freight recession, customer concentration
  (top-3 customers ~31% of revenue per the last 10-K).
- Trade press: Nimbus is closing two Midwest cross-docks (Joliet, Gary) and consolidating into a
  new Indianapolis hub; ~140 roles affected.
- Forums: unconfirmed chatter about "a strategic buyer". No named source, nothing credible. Not
  using it.

For the sector note: the unexplained volume is worth one line, but stick to what is public.
Leo asked whether to add to the position; I said the public picture is mixed and to wait for the
11/6 print.
